# 📊 Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

This project looks at customer shopping data to understand **how customers shop, what they buy, how much they spend, and what factors influence their purchasing behavior**.

I worked on the project as an end-to-end data analytics workflow, starting with cleaning the data in Python, analyzing it using SQL, and finally creating a dashboard in Power BI.

### 🔄 Project Flow

**Data Cleaning → EDA → SQL Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights**

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 customer purchase records** with **18 columns**.

Some of the main information available includes:

* Customer details such as age, gender, location, and subscription status
* Product and purchase information
* Purchase amount and product category
* Discounts and previous purchases
* Purchase frequency and review ratings
* Shipping type, season, size, and color

There were **37 missing values in the Review Rating column**, which were handled during the data-cleaning stage.

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

I used **Python and Pandas** to clean and prepare the dataset before moving on to the analysis.

Some of the main steps were:

* Loaded and explored the dataset using Pandas
* Checked the structure and summary statistics using `df.info()` and `df.describe()`
* Handled missing review ratings using the median rating for each product category
* Standardized column names using `snake_case`
* Created an `age_group` column to group customers by age
* Created a `purchase_frequency_days` column
* Checked whether `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used` contained duplicate information
* Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column
* Connected Python with PostgreSQL and loaded the cleaned data for further analysis

---

## 🗄️ SQL Analysis

After cleaning the data, I used **PostgreSQL and SQL** to answer different business questions.

The analysis included:

1. Comparing revenue generated by male and female customers
2. Finding customers who used discounts but still spent more than the average purchase amount
3. Finding the top 5 products based on average review rating
4. Comparing average purchase amounts for Standard and Express shipping
5. Comparing subscribers and non-subscribers based on spending and revenue
6. Finding products that were most dependent on discounts
7. Grouping customers into **New, Returning, and Loyal** segments
8. Finding the top 3 most purchased products in each category
9. Checking the relationship between repeat purchases and subscriptions
10. Comparing revenue generated by different age groups

## 💡 Business Insights & Recommendations

The analysis can help a business make decisions around customer retention, subscriptions, discounts, and product marketing.

Some recommendations from the analysis include:

* **Increase subscriptions:** Provide additional benefits to encourage more customers to subscribe.
* **Reward loyal customers:** Use loyalty programs to retain repeat customers and move them toward the loyal segment.
* **Review discount strategies:** Make sure discounts increase sales without unnecessarily affecting margins.
* **Promote popular products:** Use highly rated and best-selling products in marketing campaigns.
* **Use targeted marketing:** Focus campaigns on high-revenue customer groups and customers who prefer Express shipping.
